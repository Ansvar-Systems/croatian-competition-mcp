# Data Coverage — Croatian Competition MCP

## Source Authority

**AZTN — Agencija za zaštitu tržišnog natjecanja**
Croatian Competition Agency
<https://www.aztn.hr/>

## Corpus Scope

### Competition Enforcement Decisions

- **Types covered:** Abuse of dominance, cartel enforcement, sector inquiries, unfair competition
- **Jurisdiction:** Republic of Croatia
- **Legal basis:** Zakon o zaštiti tržišnog natjecanja (ZZTN) and predecessors
- **Languages:** Croatian (primary), with English summaries where available

### Merger Control Decisions

- **Outcomes covered:** Cleared, cleared with conditions, blocked, withdrawn
- **Threshold:** Croatian merger notification thresholds under ZZTN
- **Legal basis:** ZZTN merger control provisions

### Sectors

Enforcement activity is indexed across all sectors where AZTN has issued decisions, including but not limited to: energy, telecommunications, retail, financial services, healthcare, food and beverage, and transport.

## Known Gaps and Limitations

- **Historical coverage:** Historical decisions prior to AZTN's digital publication programme may be incomplete or absent.
- **Language:** Full decision texts are in Croatian. Non-Croatian speakers should treat summaries as indicative only.
- **Updates:** Database is updated periodically and may lag behind official AZTN publications by days to weeks. Use `hr_comp_check_data_freshness` to assess currency.
- **Confidential decisions:** Decisions containing business secrets may be published in redacted form or not at all.
- **Informal guidance:** AZTN comfort letters and informal guidance are not included.
- **Court appeals:** Decisions under appeal or overturned by Croatian administrative courts are not systematically flagged.

## Disclaimer

This data is provided for research purposes only and does not constitute legal advice. Always verify against primary sources at <https://www.aztn.hr/> before making compliance decisions.
